SRS Specialized Business Loan Products

Structured capital facilities tailored for Lucknow, UP and Pan-India enterprises

✔ Unsecured Business Loans

High-value business loans sanctioned without collateral or property hypothecation. Perfect for rapid cash injection, new store openings, inventory restocking, or hiring.

  • Collateral-free sanctions up to ₹75 Lakhs
  • Minimal paperwork & quick disbursal
  • Tenure: 12 to 60 Months

✔ Working Capital & OD Limits

Flexible Cash Credit (CC) and Overdraft (OD) limits that optimize your working capital cycle. Pay interest only on the exact funds utilized rather than the entire sanction.

  • Annual revolving facility with simple renewal
  • Interest calculated on daily net balance
  • Seamless liquidity cushion

✔ Term Loans

Structured long-term financing engineered for capital expenditure (CapEx), industrial plant setup, heavy machinery import, or commercial premise purchase.

  • Tenures extending up to 84 months
  • Competitive reducing interest rates
  • Structured repayment schedules

✔ Channel Financing

Strengthen dealer-distributor ecosystems. Channel financing allows distributors and suppliers to monetize trade receivables instantly, preventing cash flow bottlenecks across supply chains.

Extended credit period for dealers
Immediate cash flow for manufacturers
